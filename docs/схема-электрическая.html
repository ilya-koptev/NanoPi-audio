Connections

DC-DC (MP2307 / Mini-360)
IN+/IN− to your 12 V supply, OUT+/OUT− to the boards. It's adjustable — set OUT to 5.0 V with the trimmer before connecting (VDD_5V accepts 4.7–5.5 V).
Power → both boards
5 V (red) feeds NanoPi pin 1 (VDD_5V) and MAX98357A Vin; GND (grey) feeds NanoPi pin 12 and the amp GND.
I2S (NanoPi → amp)
LRCK→LRC, BCK→BCLK, DOUT→DIN. Three signals carry the audio; MCLK is not used.
SD — mute line
PA21→SD. Driven by the overlay (sdmode-gpios): the amp mutes when idle, which kills start/stop pops.
Bulk cap (optional)
A ~1000 µF electrolytic across 5 V/GND steadies the supply on current surges (e.g. boot). Shown grey — nice to have, not required.
Network & storage
100M Ethernet to your router (no Wi-Fi); control over the LAN — web :80, MPD :6600, MQTT :1883. Boots Armbian from the microSD.
GAIN & speaker
GAIN unconnected → 9 dB (tie to GND for up to 15 dB). Speaker 4–8 Ω across the amp's + / −.
⚠ The I2S pins are on the 12-pin header (next to USB), not the main 24-pin header.
Pin order on the MAX98357A module varies by vendor — connect by the signal name, not by position.
Solder only with the board powered off.
